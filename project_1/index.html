02 — About

A designer who codes (and vice versa)

For the past six years I've helped startups and studios turn fuzzy ideas into crisp, usable products. I care about the details most people never notice — the 200ms transition, the hierarchy of a headline, the accessibility of a color pair — because those are the details that make a product feel *considered*.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me hiking the Cascades, brewing espresso, or teaching a monthly web design workshop.
